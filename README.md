## Why this haiku?

The picture is concrete: a breakpoint that refuses to move, logs scrolling past like rain while you watch them go nowhere. The last line turns the whole poem — hours of staring at a stack trace, and the culprit is a single typo. Every word earns its place; it reads aloud like something a tired debugger would actually say at 3am.
